# User Interview — Initial Conversation With Partner

> **Status:** Early qualitative discovery. This is one conversation between the founder and their girlfriend, not validated evidence about long-distance couples in general.

## Context

The founder discussed the challenge of maintaining a healthy long-distance relationship across busy schedules with their girlfriend.

## What they agreed on

They share the view that when **both partners are busy**, the situation can feel understandable and acceptable because both know they are working hard to build a better life.

## When availability does not match

They identified a different situation: when **one partner is busy and the other is free**, the partner who is free may feel they are not getting enough attention.

## Early insight

The difficulty may not simply be that both partners have busy schedules. A more specific challenge may arise when partners' availability differs and one person wants attention or connection while the other cannot give it at that moment.

This is an early interpretation, not a confirmed root cause. The meaning of “enough attention” still needs to be explored.

## What we do not know yet

- What “enough attention” means to each partner.
- Whether the unmet need is more communication, quality time, feeling remembered, emotional connection, reassurance, or something else.
- How often this situation occurs and how much it affects the relationship.
- Whether other long-distance couples experience a similar problem.
- What the partners currently do when this happens and whether those approaches help.

## Next discovery question

**When one partner is busy and the other is free, what does the free partner feel is missing when they say they are not getting enough attention?**

Explore the real experience before proposing product features or solutions.

## Research note

Do not treat this conversation as proof that all long-distance couples have this problem. Use it to guide further interviews and investigate specific past experiences and existing coping strategies.
