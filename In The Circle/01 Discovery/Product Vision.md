# Product Vision — In The Circle

> **Status:** Founder vision — early discovery. This is not yet market-validated.

## Vision

In The Circle aims to help committed couples grow individually and together while maintaining a healthy, supportive relationship.

## Core philosophy

**Connect → Support → Grow**

- **Connect:** Stay aware of each other's everyday lives, goals, progress, and feelings.
- **Support:** Notice each other's efforts and difficulties, encourage one another, and make time for meaningful communication.
- **Grow:** Make progress toward individual goals and shared goals, develop better habits, and build a future together.

This is a reinforcing cycle: connection helps partners understand how to support each other; support helps both people keep growing; and growth gives them more to share and celebrate together.

**Two people. Two individual journeys. One shared future.**

## Intended users

The primary audience is couples in serious, committed dating relationships who want to build a future together. The product may also serve engaged and married couples who want to continue growing together.

The product is not primarily designed around casual situationships or open relationships. This describes the intended audience and product focus, not a judgment about other relationship choices.

## Intended experience

Each partner has individual goals, tasks, responsibilities, and habits. The couple can also have goals they work toward together.

The product direction is to make each partner's activities and progress visible to the other by default, so they can understand what the other is working on, what they have accomplished, and where they may be struggling. Emotional updates may help partners notice how the other is feeling and respond with care.

The intention is not to replace communication or require constant conversation. It is to give partners useful context so they can support each other through small, meaningful interactions, make time to talk when appropriate, and keep moving toward individual and shared goals.

## Desired long-term outcomes

- Both partners make progress toward their individual goals.
- They make progress toward shared goals and a common future.
- They develop better daily habits, individually and together.
- They understand and support each other's efforts and difficulties.
- They maintain a healthy relationship while pursuing their ambitions.

## Product principles to explore

1. **Mutual growth:** Personal development and shared development both matter.
2. **Mutual participation:** Both partners contribute to the relationship and shared goals.
3. **Everyday awareness:** Partners can understand what is happening in each other's lives.
4. **Meaningful communication:** Shared context can make short conversations and check-ins more thoughtful.
5. **Consistency over perfection:** An unfinished task can be continued another day; progress is a journey.
6. **Trust with consent:** Sharing may be enabled by default as a product direction, but both partners should understand and agree to what is shared.

## Important distinction

This document records the founder's intended vision and beliefs. The assumptions about other couples, their needs, and whether this approach would help them still require research and validation.
