# Discovery Stage Wrap-Up — In The Circle

> **Status:** Founder discovery completed for now. The product direction is documented, but the problem and solution are not yet validated with a wider group of couples.

## 1. Product philosophy

# Connect → Support → Grow

- **Connect:** Understand what is happening in each other's everyday lives — plans, tasks, accomplishments, difficulties, and feelings.
- **Support:** Notice each other's needs, encourage one another, recognize progress, and make time for meaningful communication.
- **Grow:** Pursue individual goals, achieve shared goals, build better habits, and maintain a healthy relationship.

The cycle is continuous: connection creates understanding, understanding enables support, and support helps both partners grow.

**Two people. Two individual journeys. One shared future.**

## 2. Intended audience

- **Primary:** Couples in serious, committed dating relationships who want to build a future together.
- **Also relevant:** Engaged and married couples who want to continue growing individually and together.

This is the founder's intended audience, not a claim that other relationship types are less valid.

## 3. What the founder wants the product to support

- Individual goals and daily tasks for each partner.
- Shared goals that both partners work toward.
- Awareness of each other's activities, plans, accomplishments, and difficulties.
- Awareness of each other's emotional state.
- Mutual encouragement and meaningful communication, even when partners are busy.
- Better habits and consistent investment in the relationship.
- Flexibility: unfinished tasks can be continued another day.

## 4. The hypothesized everyday journey

1. Each partner starts the day by reviewing their own plans and seeing what the other is working on (**Connect**).
2. Both continue their separate responsibilities and update progress (**Connect**).
3. Each notices the other's accomplishments, difficulties, or emotional state and offers encouragement or makes time to talk (**Support**).
4. Both reflect, continue unfinished work when needed, and make progress toward personal and shared goals (**Grow**).

This is a proposed journey based on the founder's thinking. It has not yet been confirmed through research with target users.

## 5. Key assumptions still to validate

- Whether couples want to share all tasks and progress by default, and what controls or boundaries they expect.
- Whether seeing a partner's tasks and feelings helps them understand each other and communicate more meaningfully.
- Whether couples want individual and shared goals in one shared space.
- Whether the main difficulties identified — different schedules, limited awareness of everyday life, planning, and unequal participation — are common and important to other couples.
- Whether the proposed Connect → Support → Grow cycle addresses a real need better than couples' current habits and tools.

## 6. Important distinction before design

The founder's philosophy and intended experience are clear enough to guide initial design exploration. However, the problem space and proposed solution remain hypotheses. Design should be used to explore and test the experience, not to treat these assumptions as proven facts.
